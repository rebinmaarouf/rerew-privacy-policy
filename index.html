1. Overview

Tyche Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") develops and operates the Rerew Fleet mobile application (Package name: com.rerew.mobile). Rerew Fleet is a specialized enterprise fleet management, telematics, and vehicle handover application designed for corporate drivers, field operators, and fleet managers. We take user privacy seriously and provide this transparent Privacy Policy to explain how location data, device permissions, and personal information are collected, used, and safeguarded.

📍 Background & Foreground Location Disclosure (Mandatory for Google Play)

Rerew Fleet collects and processes precise location data (GPS coordinates) in the foreground and in the background even when the app is closed or not in use, strictly during active driver duty shifts or vehicle dispatches. This is essential to enable real-time fleet vehicle tracking, emergency overspeed detection, automatic shift mileage calculation, route replay, and geofence safety boundary verification. Location tracking immediately ceases once the driver ends their shift or logs out.

2. Information We Collect

  • Precise Location Data: Foreground and background GPS telemetry (latitude, longitude, speed, heading, and altitude) recorded during active driver shifts.
  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, corporate email address, employee/driver code, phone number, and branch assignment assigned by your company administrator.
  • Camera & Photos Access: Used solely and exclusively when the driver chooses to take or upload photos of vehicle handover condition, odometer readings, fuel purchase receipts, maintenance inspections, or road accident reports.
  • Device & Notification Telemetry: Device manufacturer, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM/Expo) push tokens required to send critical operational dispatch alerts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time GPS tracking of enterprise vehicles on the supervisor dashboard.
  • To compute accurate fuel consumption, mileage logs, and vehicle handover reports.
  • To dispatch emergency notifications regarding safety violations (e.g. overspeeding, boundary exit, accident alerts).
  • To verify driver presence at assigned branch locations or delivery hubs.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or location data to advertising brokers, marketing networks, or third-party data analytics firms. Data is only accessible to authorized supervisors and company management within your organization.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communications between the Rerew Fleet mobile application and our servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Stored data is maintained in secure, monitored enterprise database environments with role-based access control.

6. User Data Deletion & Retention

We retain driver shift logs and telematics data only for as long as required by your employer's fleet audit regulations. Drivers and users have the right to request access to or complete deletion of their personal account and associated telemetry data at any time by contacting our data protection officer at rebin.maaruf@gmail.com. Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
